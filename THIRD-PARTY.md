# Third-party components

- [PeerJS](https://github.com/peers/peerjs) — MIT. Its public PeerServer is used for signaling.
- [QRCode](https://github.com/soldair/node-qrcode) — MIT.
- [JSZip](https://github.com/Stuk/jszip) — MIT or GPLv3 (used under MIT).
- [Vite](https://github.com/vitejs/vite) — MIT.
- [esbuild](https://github.com/evanw/esbuild) — MIT.
- [MindAR](https://github.com/hiukim/mind-ar-js) — MIT. Pinned version 1.2.5 is loaded from jsDelivr for compilation and AR.
- [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) — MIT. Pinned version 0.160.0 is loaded from jsDelivr for AR.
- DM Sans and Playfair Display — SIL Open Font License, supplied through Google Fonts with system fallbacks.

The sample landscape in `public/sample-painting.svg` is original illustrative artwork supplied with this project. It is a still image and is not presented as an AI-generated video.
